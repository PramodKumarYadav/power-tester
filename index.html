About Me

Who am I?

A passionate Test Automation Expert running a one-man software testing company.

Hi there 👋, my name is Pramod Yadav (a.k.a. Power Tester). I am a freelance Test Automation Expert (ZZP) and Power Tester is my one-man software testing company.

I have a little over 20 years of experience in testing and building end-to-end test automation solutions for both enterprise and startup customers. Some well-known enterprise customers include ING, ABN AMRO, Rabobank, European Patent Office, Mercell and Atradius. Fast-paced startup clients include Felyx and ClipBoardHealth.

I enjoy working with both startups and enterprises as they come with their own unique challenges and strengths.

I also offer free 20-minute sparring sessions for the QA community — covering automation, career growth, and test strategies.
