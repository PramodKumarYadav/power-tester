About Pramod Yadav

Freelance Test Automation Expert (ZZP) with 20+ years of experience.

Hi there 👋, my name is Pramod Yadav and I am a freelance Test Automation Expert (ZZP). Power Tester is my one-man software testing company.

I have a little over 20 years of experience in building end-to-end test automation solutions for both enterprise and startup customers. Some of my well-known enterprise customers include ING, ABN AMRO, Rabobank, European Patent Office and Atradius. Startup customers include Mercell, Felyx and ClipBoardHealth.

Skills & Experience

Test Automation

Frontend (monolith, micro-frontend, web and mobile), backend (microservices, REST and SOAP) and databases (SQL and NoSQL). My tools of choice: Playwright (TypeScript) for web, RestAssured (Java) for API.

CI/CD Integration

Solid experience with Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions and Jenkins, integrating tests closely into CI/CD pipelines for fast, reliable feedback loops.

Test Monitoring

Experience with ElasticSearch, Kibana, DataDog and BrowserStack Test Monitoring to derive meaningful metrics from test runs.

Leadership

Hands-on experience leading, mentoring, and scaling test automation teams. Creating test strategies and putting them into action at both startup and enterprise scale.

Motto: Learn, Share, Have Fun!
